Genus & type species
Taxonomic treatment & nomenclature

Type material (holotype, allotype, paratypes) is entered in each species tab.

Morphological text boxes contain editable example wording or bracketed prompts. Taxon-specific facts (names, authorship, LSIDs, type data, and measurements) remain blank to avoid inventing information. Replace these with specimen-based observations; bracketed prompts are automatically excluded from the generated description. For each character, choose a state, a range, or alternatives. Presence or absence can be entered directly in the state field (e.g. present, absent, residual, or vestigial). End a text with a full stop to start a new sentence; otherwise clauses join with semicolons.
